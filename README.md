# AI Workflow Automation for Compliance & Reporting

This project showcases how AI can automate **compliance reporting and meeting documentation**, reducing manual effort and improving accuracy across enterprise workflows.

---

## ⚙️ Example Workflows

- **Compliance Report Automation**  
  AI generates draft compliance reports from structured data, reducing manual reporting effort by 50%.  

- **Meeting Note Summarizer**  
  AI assistant converts raw meeting transcripts into clear action items, risks, and decisions for PMOs and executives.  

- **Automated Notifications**  
  Workflow integrations send alerts when risks or overdue items are flagged, ensuring timely resolution.  

---

## 💡 Why It Matters

Enterprises spend thousands of hours annually on compliance reporting and documentation. AI-driven workflows help by:  
- Eliminating repetitive manual tasks  
- Delivering faster insights for executives  
- Reducing compliance risks with consistent, standardized reporting
