# AI Workflow Automation for Compliance & Reporting
This project showcases how AI can automate **compliance reporting and meeting documentation**, reducing manual effort and improving accuracy across enterprise workflows.

---
## 💡 Why It Matters

Enterprises spend thousands of hours annually on compliance reporting and documentation. AI-driven workflows help by:  
- Streamlining repetitive manual reporting tasks
- Improving accuracy with consistent AI-enabled outputs
- Providing executives with faster access to compliance insights and alerts
